## Scheduler Algorithm

`buildSessionSequence` uses a 4-step greedy interleave:

1. Place ready test (gap >= minIntroToTestGap satisfied)
2. Place due review (D-19: reviews precede new intros)
3. Introduce new card (queues pending test)
4. Fallback: emit unmet-lag tests (RESEARCH Pitfall 4 — small-pool case)

CONTEXT-LOCKs D-17/D-18/D-19/D-20 documented in module header. O(n×m) complexity
is acceptable for pool sizes <=30 per session (D-17).
