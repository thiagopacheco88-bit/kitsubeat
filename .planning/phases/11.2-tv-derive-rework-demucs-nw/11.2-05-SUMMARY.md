## Surprise findings (worth flagging upstream)

1. **6 youtube-id dupes in `data/songs-manifest-tv.json`**, affecting 10 slugs. Plan author had no visibility into this. Plan 11.1 / Add-Song Pipeline likely owns the cleanup.
2. **2 mismatched full youtube-ids** in `data/songs-manifest.json` (remember-flow, shinkokyuu-super-beaver). Could indicate broader manifest reliability issues; worth a full audit.
3. **WhisperX memory leak** in `tv-transcribe-stems.py` — model reloads per song without freeing prior memory; mkl_malloc fails after ~20 consecutive songs. Required 3 process restarts to clear the batch. Documented as a followup.
4. **Demucs faster than expected** — ~50s/track on this machine vs. 5min/track CPU baseline (likely GPU-accelerated under the hood).
