# Phase 18.1 Plan 06: Journal PT-BR Translations Batch 1 Summary

**One-liner:** 10 journal articles translated to naturalistic Brazilian Portuguese with verbatim Japanese content preservation across anime/language topics.

## What Was Built

Created `src/content/journal/pt-BR/` directory and translated 10 EN journal articles to Brazilian Portuguese MDX files. Each translation:

- Preserves Japanese text (kanji, kana, romaji, lyrics blocks, vocabulary Japanese columns, grammar examples) verbatim
- Translates all prose, section headings, FAQ questions and answers, article titles and subtitles, vocabulary meaning columns
- Includes `locale: pt-BR` in frontmatter
- Uses the same `slug:` as the EN source (enabling cross-locale article lookup)
- Uses the same `date:` as the EN source
- Is written in naturalistic Brazilian Portuguese (not literal translation)
