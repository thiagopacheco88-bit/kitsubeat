# Phase 18.1 Plan 09: Spanish Journal Articles Batch 1 Summary

10 Spanish (ES) MDX journal article translations created covering articles 1-10. All prose translated to neutral Latin American Spanish; all Japanese content preserved verbatim.

## What Was Built

Created `src/content/journal/es/` directory with 10 MDX files translating the first 10 EN journal articles to Spanish:

1. **anime-counting-japanese-numbers.mdx** — Numbers in anime (Hokage titles, Yonko, Sannin, etc.)
2. **ninja-vs-samurai-japanese-history.mdx** — Historical ninja vs samurai class divide, 35 vocabulary words
3. **japanese-days-of-week-anime-mnemonics.mdx** — Japanese weekday elements mapped to anime characters
4. **gurenge-demon-slayer-japanese-lesson.mdx** — Gurenge by LiSA, grammar (hodo, tame ni, te-form)
5. **unravel-tokyo-ghoul-japanese-lesson.mdx** — Unravel by TK, grammar (kakeru, te+ii, de wa ienai)
6. **we-are-one-piece-japanese-lesson.mdx** — We Are! by Hiroshi Kitadani, grammar (you ni, made, imperatives)
7. **silhouette-naruto-japanese-lesson.mdx** — Silhouette by KANA-BOON, grammar (te iku, tsuzukeru, eba ii na)
8. **again-fullmetal-alchemist-japanese-lesson.mdx** — Again by YUI, grammar (te ita, sou, mata vs mada)
9. **cha-la-head-cha-la-dragon-ball-japanese-lesson.mdx** — Cha-La Head-Cha-La, Japanese onomatopoeia (giseigo/gitaigo)
10. **zankoku-na-tenshi-no-thesis-japanese-lesson.mdx** — Cruel Angel's Thesis, grammar (temo, you ni, imperatives)

## Translation Approach

- **Prose**: Fully translated to neutral Latin American Spanish (no vosotros, prefers ustedes)
- **Japanese content**: All kanji, kana, romaji, grammar examples, and vocabulary tables preserved verbatim
- **Frontmatter**: title, subtitle, description, summary, FAQ — all translated; slug, date, tags, author, readingTime unchanged; `locale: es` added
- **Vocabulary tables**: Column headers translated ("Meaning" → "Significado", "What it counts" → "Qué cuenta", etc.)
- **Links**: Internal links (/songs, /journal) preserved in Spanish prose context
