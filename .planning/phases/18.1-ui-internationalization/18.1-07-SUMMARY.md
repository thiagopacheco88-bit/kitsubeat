# Phase 18.1 Plan 07: Journal PT-BR Translations Batch 2 Summary

## What Was Built

Translated 10 EN journal articles to Brazilian Portuguese MDX files, continuing the pattern established in Plan 06. Each translation:

- Preserves Japanese text (kanji, kana, romaji, lyrics blocks, vocabulary tables, grammar examples) verbatim
- Preserves katakana loanwords verbatim (ミルク, コーヒー, ゲーム etc. in the katakana article)
- Translates all prose, section headings, FAQ questions and answers, article titles and subtitles, vocabulary meaning columns
- Includes `locale: pt-BR` in frontmatter
- Uses the same `slug:` as the EN source (enabling cross-locale article lookup)
- Uses the same `date:` as the EN source
- Is written in naturalistic Brazilian Portuguese (not literal translation)
